## 55. what is an integrated assessment model?

### Integrated assessment model (IAM)

An integrated assessment model combines knowledge from two or more domains in one consistent framework. In climate analysis, IAMs commonly connect parts of the economy and energy system with land use, greenhouse-gas emissions and a representation of the climate system. Researchers use them to explore how socioeconomic and technological pathways, policies and climate outcomes interact.

IAM results are conditional scenarios, not a single prediction: they depend on the model structure and the assumptions supplied to it.
